# Elsevier acquisition of Shadow Health — December 2020

## What this is

Public acquisition announcement and supporting university reporting for Elsevier's purchase of Shadow Health.

## Sources

- Elsevier announcement distributed through PR Newswire, **“Elsevier acquires Shadow Health, a leading provider of best-in-class healthcare simulations”**, December 9, 2020: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/elsevier-acquires-shadow-health-a-leading-provider-of-best-in-class-healthcare-simulations-301189121.html
- University of Florida CISE reproduction/background: https://cise.ufl.edu/2021/01/shadow-health-co-founded-by-cise-professor-is-acquired-by-elsevier/
- UF Innovate acquisition notice: https://innovate.research.ufl.edu/elsevier-acquires-shadow-health/
- Secondary transaction database for deal terms: https://www.cbinsights.com/company/shadow-health

## Durable observations

- Elsevier announced the acquisition on December 9, 2020.
- At acquisition, Elsevier described Shadow Health as a Florida-based developer of virtual simulations in nursing and healthcare education and said its products were used in more than 900 institutions across North America.
- The acquisition rationale emphasized digital education, clinical practice readiness, communication, clinical reasoning, and the increased importance of virtual simulation during the pandemic and growth of telehealth.
- Jan Herzhoff framed the acquisition in terms of supporting nurses from education into practice; Brent Gordon framed it around learning outcomes and practice readiness.
- The acquisition followed Elsevier's launch of Transition to Practice, which reinforces that Shadow Health was being placed inside a broader education-to-practice strategy rather than treated as a stand-alone simulation asset.
- Public secondary transaction sources report that financial terms were not disclosed. No reliable public purchase price was located in this research pass.

## Extracted knowledge

Supports the acquisition-history claim and the broader interpretation of Shadow Health's strategic role inside Elsevier Health.
