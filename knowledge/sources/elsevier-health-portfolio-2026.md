# Elsevier Health portfolio and operating structure

## What this is

Current public Health market overview plus recent official product and leadership announcements.

## Provenance

## Raw notes or excerpt

Elsevier Health publicly groups offerings across medical education, nursing education, clinical decisions and patient engagement. Featured education products include ClinicalKey Student, Complete Anatomy, Osmosis, Clinical Cases, Sherpath, Sherpath AI, Shadow Health and HESI. Clinical products include ClinicalKey, ClinicalKey AI, Clinical Pharmacology and ClinicalPath.

Jan Herzhoff is President of Health Markets. Omry Bigger is President of Clinical Solutions. Brent Gordon is President of Global Healthcare Education.

Recent 2026 additions/launches include Mytonomy patient engagement, Wellsheet EHR workflow integration, Shadow Health Lab with VR, Osmosis AI, HESI Personalized Learning Plan, Nora AI and expanded ClinicalKey AI/Ask AI.
