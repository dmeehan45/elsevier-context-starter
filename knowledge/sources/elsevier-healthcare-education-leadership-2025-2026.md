# Elsevier Healthcare Education leadership signals

## What this is

Public sources clarifying current Healthcare Education leadership and Paul Crockett's visible remit.

## Provenance

Accessed 2026-09-14.

## Raw notes or excerpt

Elsevier appointed Brent Gordon President of its global Healthcare Education business in September 2025. The unit serves nursing, health and medical education and publicly names Sherpath AI, ClinicalKey Student, Shadow Health, HESI, Complete Anatomy and Osmosis as a unified portfolio.

Paul Crockett's public title is inconsistent across live Elsevier pages. An Elsevier Education author bio identifies him as Senior Vice President of Product and Enterprise Solutions and says his team focuses on nursing and health professions products and AI. A current Elsevier ANZ educator webinar promotes him as "Chief AI Officer at Elsevier." This should be treated as a current-title ambiguity, not silently resolved.
