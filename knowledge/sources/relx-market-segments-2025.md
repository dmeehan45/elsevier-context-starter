# RELX market segments and 2025 financial scale

## What this is

RELX's official market-segment overview and 2025 annual-report data.

## Provenance

Accessed 2026-09-14. Supporting annual-report page: https://www.relx.com/investors/annual-reports/2025

## Raw notes or excerpt

RELX reports four operating business areas: Risk; Scientific, Technical & Medical; Legal; and Exhibitions, with print and print-related activities reported separately from 2025. RELX identifies Elsevier with the Scientific, Technical & Medical business.

For 2025, Scientific, Technical & Medical reported revenue of £2.714 billion and adjusted operating profit of £1.035 billion.

RELX's business labels map to operating brands as follows: Risk to LexisNexis Risk Solutions, Scientific/Technical/Medical to Elsevier, Legal to LexisNexis Legal & Professional, and Exhibitions to RX.
