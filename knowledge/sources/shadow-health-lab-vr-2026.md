# Shadow Health Lab with Virtual Reality — 2026

## What this is

Elsevier's June 2026 launch announcement and current product page for the expanded Shadow Health Lab product.

## Sources

- Launch announcement: https://www.elsevier.com/about/press-releases/elsevier-launches-shadow-health-lab-with-virtual-reality-to-transform-clinical-education-for-nursing-students
- Product page: https://www.elsevier.com/products/shadow-health-lab
- Nursing-education portfolio context: https://www.elsevier.com/solutions/nursing-education

## Durable observations

Shadow Health Lab with Virtual Reality expands the Shadow Health brand beyond its original individual, screen-based DCE format.

Current product claims include:

- 165+ evidence-based lab simulations;
- 30 unique VR patient encounters spanning the undergraduate nursing curriculum;
- a multi-patient VR scenario focused on prioritization and coordination of care;
- structured preparation, facilitation, debriefing, remediation, and performance reporting;
- Osmosis videos and SimChart Next Generation EHR activities integrated into pre/post simulation work;
- the Shadow Health Conversation Engine extended into VR encounters;
- support for use alongside high-fidelity simulation labs and, depending on program/regulatory context, as a substitute for some clinical hours.

## Strategic interpretation

This launch is material because it shifts Shadow Health from being primarily a screen-based virtual-patient product toward a broader **patient-encounter and simulation platform** spanning asynchronous virtual patients, facilitated lab simulation, EHR practice, and immersive VR.

That interpretation is synthesis, not an explicit company taxonomy.
