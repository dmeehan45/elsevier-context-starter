# Shadow Health competitive landscape — 2026 source record

## What this is

Official public product pages for major products that compete with, overlap with, or substitute for Shadow Health in nursing and healthcare simulation.

## Direct and adjacent competitors

### Wolters Kluwer / Laerdal — vSim for Nursing and Lippincott Practice

- https://www.wolterskluwer.com/en/solutions/lippincott-nursing-faculty/vsim-for-nursing
- https://www.wolterskluwer.com/en/solutions/lippincott-nursing-faculty/simulations

vSim is co-developed by Wolters Kluwer and Laerdal, with scenarios authored with the National League for Nursing. Wolters Kluwer reports more than two million nursing students have experienced vSim. Lippincott Practice increasingly bundles vSim with VR, EHR/documentation, patient cases, and other clinical-practice learning products.

### Kaplan — i-Human Patients

- https://kaplan.com/institutions-educators/higher-education/nursing-schools/i-human

i-Human spans undergraduate/graduate nursing plus medical, osteopathic, physician-associate, athletic-therapy, hospital-system, and international medical audiences. Kaplan positions current differentiation around full case simulation, analytics, curriculum integration, and an AI Tutor that provides individualized feedback.

### Sentinel U

- https://www.sentinelu.com/
- https://www.sentinelu.com/learn

Sentinel U is nursing-focused and combines virtual clinicals with curricular alignment resources, debriefing tools, and performance analytics. It spans undergraduate through advanced practice and professional development.

### Oxford Medical Simulation

- https://oxfordmedicalsimulation.com/

OMS is strongly VR/immersive oriented and markets AI, analytics, scenario practice, educator services, and use across academic programs and health systems.

### Body Interact

- https://bodyinteract.com/
- https://bodyinteract.com/nursing/

Body Interact emphasizes dynamic physiology models, responsive virtual patients, clinical reasoning, analytics, and cross-professional use.

### ATI — Real Life / Swift River

- https://www.atitesting.com/educator/solutions/real-life/scenarios
- https://www.atitesting.com/educator/outcomes/clinical-experience

ATI offers live-actor clinical-reasoning simulations through Real Life and a large screen-based scenario library through Swift River, embedded in a broader nursing assessment and readiness ecosystem.

## Competitive interpretation

The category is converging. Vendors increasingly compete not on a single virtual-patient experience but on how simulation connects to curriculum, assessment, analytics, EHR/documentation, VR, faculty workflow, and AI-supported remediation.

That last statement is synthesis from the cited product portfolios.
