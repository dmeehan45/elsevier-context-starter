## Current product observations

Shadow Health Digital Clinical Experiences are high-fidelity, screen-based simulations in which learners interact with Digital Standardized Patients. The core interaction model includes:

- interviewing and therapeutic communication using the learner's own words through the patented Conversation Engine;
- physical examination and collection of objective findings;
- EHR/documentation practice;
- education and empathy actions;
- clinical reasoning and information-processing activities;
- reflection and feedback;
- scoring and proficiency feedback.

Elsevier currently states that the product contains 80+ diverse virtual patients capable of responding to millions of possible questions and is used by more than 700,000 nursing students. The product page does not make the denominator/time period of that reach metric sufficiently precise to treat it as a verified annual-active-user count.

## Curricular coverage

Current pages position Shadow Health primarily around nursing:

- undergraduate nursing across 11 collections spanning areas such as fundamentals, health assessment, leadership, mental health, medical-surgical, maternal health, pediatrics, pharmacology, gerontology, community health, and pathophysiology;
- graduate nursing across eight collections, including advanced health assessment, pathophysiology, pharmacology, diagnostics, adult primary care, mental health, pediatrics, and women's reproductive health;
- novice/new graduate nurses in healthcare organizations.

## Buyer and access model

Public marketing is aimed at nursing faculty, deans/directors, and institutions. Evolve legal terms show a hybrid B2B2C adoption model: institutions/faculty adopt and configure the product for courses, while students may purchase or redeem access codes/enrollment for required modules.
