# Nursing program technology integration gaps to validate internally

Public research establishes the broad architecture but not the actual integration state of Shadow Health customers. The following should be validated with implementation, support, product analytics and customer-success data.

## Customer stack distribution

- What percentage of active institutions use Canvas, Blackboard, Brightspace, Moodle or another LMS?
- What percentage use Shadow Health LTI 1.3 versus Evolve/direct access or legacy integration paths?
- What share have grade passback/deep linking/course provisioning enabled?
- What clinical-management platforms are most common among customers: Exxat, CastleBranch/DISA, Typhon, others?
- What physical-simulation management systems are common: SimCapture, LearningSpace, others?

## Manual-work burden

- Which tasks still require faculty to enter the same information twice?
- How often do faculty manually transfer grades or use CSV export/import?
- What support tickets are caused by LMS roster/section/course-copy mismatches?
- How much faculty time is spent configuring dates, sections, assignments and remediation options each term?
- Are multi-campus programs doing repeated local configuration after a central adoption?

## Staff roles and permissions

- Which roles actually use Shadow Health today: individual course faculty, course coordinators, simulation staff, program directors, deans, assessment/accreditation staff, instructional designers, IT/LMS admins?
- Do these roles need materially different views, reports or permissions?
- Who owns implementation after the initial vendor onboarding period?

## Evidence interoperability

- Do customers attempt to combine Shadow Health data with LMS, Exxat/Typhon, SimCapture, accreditation or BI data?
- Which data fields are used outside Shadow Health today?
- Which exports/APIs/integrations are requested most often?
- What institution-defined learner identifiers could safely connect evidence across systems?
- What privacy/governance constraints limit longitudinal learner linking?

## Technology and adoption maturity

- Does depth of Shadow Health use correlate with dedicated instructional design/simulation/IT support?
- Do faculty training completion and implementation-support intensity predict usage or renewal?
- Are support burdens materially different between LTI-integrated and non-integrated customers?
- Are there identifiable 'stack archetypes' that predict implementation friction?

## Strategic question

If Elsevier wants Shadow Health to become a longitudinal practice/readiness layer, what is the minimum interoperability needed to make its evidence useful across the broader program without trying to replace the LMS, clinical-management system or simulation-center stack?
