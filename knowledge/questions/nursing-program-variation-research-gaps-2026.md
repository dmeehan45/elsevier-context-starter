# Open questions: nursing-program variation and Shadow Health buying systems

## Highest-priority validation questions

### Account reality versus public governance model

- In current Shadow Health accounts, who actually initiates purchase, controls budget, signs off academically, implements, and decides renewal?
- How often is the dean/nurse administrator the economic buyer versus a health-sciences dean, central academic unit, procurement-led contract, or student-paid model?
- Which stakeholder most strongly predicts implementation depth after contract signature?

### Campus versus system

- For multi-campus customers, is Shadow Health contracted at course, campus, school, university or system level?
- When campuses use the same curriculum, how much does Shadow Health configuration differ by campus?
- Does usage variance correlate with local clinical placement scarcity, simulation staffing, faculty onboarding, or campus leadership?

### Simulation and clinical placement

- Which jurisdictions and accreditors recognize which forms of virtual simulation toward practice/clinical hours, and under what conditions?
- How often do customers actually use Shadow Health as formal clinical-hour substitution versus supplemental practice?
- Are customers able to demonstrate transfer from Shadow Health performance to later clinical performance?

### Leadership and simulation maturity

- When a school has a senior simulation leader, does that role champion virtual simulation or protect a physical-simulation-centered operating model?
- What distinguishes programs where simulation leadership reports into academic affairs versus clinical education versus operations?
- Does a dedicated simulation center increase adoption or raise the evidence/integration bar enough to make adoption harder?

### Procurement and AI

- Which institutional AI review processes now sit on top of ordinary educational-technology procurement?
- What documentation most often stalls or accelerates Shadow Health procurement: accessibility, security, data retention, model behavior, LMS/SSO, contracting, evidence?
- Are renewals subject to the same reviews as new purchases when AI functionality materially changes?

### International market fit

- Which countries allow virtual/simulated practice to count toward mandated clinical/practice hours and which explicitly exclude it?
- Where are nursing curricula nationally standardized enough to enable centralized content mapping?
- How do procurement authority and clinical partnerships differ in publicly funded national health systems versus fragmented U.S. markets?

## Research discipline

Do not turn missing answers into assumed market truths. Public organizational charts tell us formal structure; they do not reveal informal influence, actual contract authority, implementation behavior, or renewal politics.

The next validation layer should use internal account/CRM evidence, interviews with sales/customer-success/product teams, and direct customer interviews to test the public-research model.
