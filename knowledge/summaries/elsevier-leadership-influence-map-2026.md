# Elsevier leadership and influence map — 2026

This page separates **formal company leadership** from **role-relevant influence**. The second category is analytical: it means "likely important to understand for product, AI and Health context," not "higher in an unpublished internal power hierarchy."

## Parent-company layer

- **Erik Engstrom — CEO, RELX.** Group-level strategy matters because RELX explicitly frames long-term growth around analytics and decision tools that combine proprietary content/data with advanced technology and AI.
- **Kumsal Bayazit — CEO, Elsevier / CEO, RELX Scientific, Technical & Medical.** The central executive for Elsevier. Her background includes RELX strategy and other divisions, so she links Elsevier to group-level operating logic.
- **Youngsuk "YS" Chi — Chair, Elsevier; Director of RELX Corporate Affairs.** Important bridge across Elsevier, RELX and the research/publishing ecosystem.

## Elsevier enterprise leadership to know

Current public leadership includes:

- **Jamie Buckley — Chief Product Officer**
- **Jill Luber — Chief Technology Officer**
- **Dr Kieran West — EVP, Strategy**
- **Judy Verses — President, Academic and Government**
- **Stuart Whayman — President, Corporate Markets**
- **Laura Hassink — President, Journals**
- plus executive leaders for finance, operations, HR, legal, communications and corporate affairs.

For product work, Buckley, Luber and West are particularly important context because they sit at the product/technology/strategy intersection across Elsevier rather than inside one market vertical.

## Health leadership

- **Jan Herzhoff — President, Health Markets.** Top public operating leader for Elsevier Health.
- **Brent Gordon — President, Global Healthcare Education.** Leads the combined nursing, health-professions and medical-education unit. Elsevier's 2025 appointment announcement explicitly put Shadow Health, HESI, Sherpath AI, ClinicalKey Student, Complete Anatomy and Osmosis into his portfolio.
- **Omry Bigger — President, Clinical Solutions.** Leads the clinical decision-support business. His background includes commercial leadership of Lexis+ AI at another RELX division, making him a useful signal of cross-RELX AI operating patterns.
- **Paul Crockett — Healthcare Education product/AI leader.** Former Authess co-founder/CEO. Public pages currently differ on title: SVP Product and Enterprise Solutions versus Chief AI Officer. The stable takeaway is remit, not title: product, assessment and AI applied to nursing/health-professions outcomes.

## Influence map for Shadow Health context

For a product leader working on Shadow Health, a practical public-context ladder is:

**RELX strategy → Kumsal Bayazit → Jan Herzhoff → Brent Gordon → Healthcare Education product/AI leadership → Shadow Health team.**

The adjacent executive worth understanding is **Omry Bigger**, because Clinical Solutions is where Elsevier is pushing AI into live clinical decision workflow, while Healthcare Education is pushing AI and simulation into learner readiness. Those businesses solve different jobs, but the design patterns around trusted evidence, workflow integration and measurable outcomes are increasingly parallel.

## Watch-outs

- Public leadership pages are a lagging representation of internal organization changes.
- Do not infer reporting lines beyond what Elsevier states publicly.
- Paul Crockett's current public title is inconsistent across Elsevier properties; verify internally before using a formal title.
- "Influence" here is role-based relevance, not a claim about politics or informal power.
