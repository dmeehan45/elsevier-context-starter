# Elsevier acquisition history — strategy-shaping timeline

This is a **selected strategic timeline**, not a complete M&A database. The inclusion test is whether the acquired capability still helps explain Elsevier's current portfolio or operating model.

| Year | Acquisition | Capability added / strategic significance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2001 | Harcourt STM and Education/Testing businesses | Major expansion of scientific/medical and education assets; an important foundation for later Health and education scale. |
| 2012 | Atira | Research information management; strengthens institutional research workflow and Pure. |
| 2013 | Mendeley | Researcher reference management, collaboration and workflow beyond content access. |
| 2016 | SSRN | Preprints, early-research distribution and scholarly community. |
| 2017 | bepress | Institutional repositories and scholarly communication; Digital Commons. |
| 2018 | Aries Systems | Manuscript submission, peer review and publication workflow infrastructure. |
| 2019 | 3D4Medical | 3D anatomy learning; Complete Anatomy. |
| 2020 | Authess | Performance-based competency assessment focused on how learners solve complex problems, not only answer tests. |
| 2020 | SciBite | Semantic AI, ontologies and text/data intelligence for life sciences and R&D. |
| 2020 | Shadow Health | Virtual patient simulation for nursing and health-professions education. |
| 2021 | Osmosis | Visual, learner-centered healthcare education and assessment content. |
| 2022 | Interfolio | Faculty information, academic hiring/review/promotion and research-administration workflow. |
| 2026 | Mytonomy | Patient-engagement platform, multimedia education and patient-journey communications. |
| 2026 | Wellsheet | Patient-context aggregation from EHRs and clinical workflow integration at the point of care. |

## What the sequence says

The repeated move is not simply "buy more content." The acquired assets occupy adjacent moments in a professional workflow:

**discover knowledge → organize work → assess performance → simulate decisions → support real-world decisions → engage the patient.**

In research, the equivalent expansion is:

**read/publish → manage references → share early work → run repositories → manage research/faculty information → use semantic/AI systems across discovery and R&D.**

This makes the most useful strategic reading of Elsevier a **workflow-expansion thesis**: trusted content and data are the base layer, while acquisitions add the places where work is performed and decisions are made. This is an analytical synthesis, not an official statement of acquisition doctrine.

## Particularly relevant lineage for Healthcare Education

**Harcourt → 3D4Medical → Authess → Shadow Health → Osmosis** creates a portfolio spanning content, visualization, competency assessment, simulation and learner support. By 2025 Elsevier publicly grouped Shadow Health, HESI, Sherpath AI, ClinicalKey Student, Complete Anatomy and Osmosis under one Global Healthcare Education leader.

## Current outward edge of Health

The 2026 Mytonomy and Wellsheet acquisitions sit outside education but matter strategically: they extend Elsevier Health deeper into patient engagement and the live clinical workflow. That creates potential adjacency between what Elsevier knows about learner readiness and what its Clinical Solutions business knows about real-world clinical decisions, even though the public materials do not claim that these portfolios share a single learner/clinician model.
