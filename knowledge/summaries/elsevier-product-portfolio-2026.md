# Elsevier product portfolio map — 2026

This is a navigational map, not an exhaustive SKU catalog. Products are grouped by the user workflow they serve, because that is more useful than alphabetic listing.

## Research publishing, discovery and researcher workflow

- **ScienceDirect** — full-text scientific, technical and health literature.
- **Scopus** — publisher-neutral abstract and citation database.
- **Mendeley** — reference management and researcher workflow.
- **SSRN** — preprint and early-research network.
- **LeapSpace** — research-grade AI workspace spanning literature exploration, synthesis, writing support, comparison, funding and collaborator discovery.
- **Journals and books** — core publishing franchises including Elsevier journals, The Lancet group and Cell Press.

## Research intelligence and institutional workflow

- **SciVal** — research performance analytics and benchmarking.
- **Pure** — research information management system.
- **Digital Commons** — institutional repositories and scholarly communication.
- **Interfolio** — faculty information, hiring, review/promotion/tenure and academic workflow.
- **Researchfish** — research-outcomes and impact tracking for funders and institutions.

## Corporate and scientific R&D

- **Reaxys** — chemistry data and small-molecule research.
- **Embase** — biomedical literature database.
- **Engineering Village** — engineering search/discovery.
- **Knovel** — technical references and materials-property data.
- **PharmaPendium** — drug-safety/efficacy and regulatory data.
- **SciBite** — semantic data, text analytics and ontology management.
- **LeapSpace / ScienceDirect / Scopus** also cross into corporate R&D workflows.

## Healthcare Education

The 2025-26 public framing is increasingly unified across nursing, health professions and medicine:

- **Sherpath / Sherpath AI** — courseware and adaptive/AI-supported learning.
- **Shadow Health** — virtual-patient simulation; 2026 extension into Shadow Health Lab with VR.
- **HESI** — assessment, exam readiness, analytics and clinical-practice readiness.
- **ClinicalKey Student** — medical-education reference/content.
- **Complete Anatomy** — 3D anatomy learning from the 3D4Medical acquisition.
- **Osmosis / Osmosis AI** — visual learning, retrieval practice and AI study support.
- **Clinical Cases** — clinical-reasoning learning.
- **Nora AI** — AI learning support embedded in Elsevier healthcare eBooks through VitalSource Bookshelf.

## Clinical Solutions and patient workflow

- **ClinicalKey / ClinicalKey AI** — evidence-based clinical decision support.
- **Clinical Pharmacology** — drug information.
- **ClinicalPath** — oncology decision support and pathways.
- **Mytonomy** — patient engagement, education and communications integrated with health-system workflows.
- **Wellsheet** — EHR data aggregation and patient context intended to combine with Elsevier evidence inside clinical workflow.

## Portfolio pattern to remember

Elsevier is better understood as a set of **information + workflow + decision-support systems** than as a publishing company with add-on software. Publishing remains a critical content and trust asset, but many growth products sit at the point where a researcher, learner, educator or clinician has to decide what to do next.
