# Elsevier corporate and ownership map — 2026

## The shortest useful model

**RELX PLC → RELX Group plc → operating businesses.** Elsevier is RELX's Scientific, Technical & Medical business, not a separately listed company.

RELX's four operating businesses are:

| RELX area | Primary operating brand | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Risk | LexisNexis Risk Solutions | Risk, identity, fraud, insurance and business decision tools |
| Scientific, Technical & Medical | Elsevier | Science, research, healthcare education and clinical decision support |
| Legal | LexisNexis Legal & Professional | Legal research, workflow and analytics |
| Exhibitions | RX | B2B exhibitions and marketplaces |

RELX PLC is the sole parent company and owns 100% of RELX Group plc. RELX PLC shares trade in London and Amsterdam, with ADSs in New York.

## Where Elsevier sits economically

For 2025, RELX reported Scientific, Technical & Medical revenue of **£2.714bn** and adjusted operating profit of **£1.035bn**. From 2025 RELX changed segment reporting so print/print-related activities are separated and a small commercial-healthcare portfolio moved to Risk; older segment comparisons therefore need normalization.

## Shareholder nuance

RELX is a public company, so an Elsevier "owner" is best understood as the RELX corporate chain rather than a single private sponsor. RELX's 2025 annual report disclosed substantial-interest notifications for BlackRock and Invesco, but explicitly warned that the notified percentages may not reflect holdings at year-end because the notifications were older. Do not treat those disclosures as a live cap table.

## Strategic implication

Elsevier benefits from being inside a parent group that has parallel businesses in legal AI/workflow, risk analytics and exhibitions. Cross-RELX executive movement is common: Elsevier CEO Kumsal Bayazit previously held RELX strategy and other divisional roles; Clinical Solutions President Omry Bigger came from LexisNexis Legal & Professional. That makes RELX a relevant source of operating patterns and leadership talent, not merely a financial parent.
