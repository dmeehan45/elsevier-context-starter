# Contribution: nursing program technology environment

Public-source research and synthesis covering the technology backdrop on the staff/program side of nursing education:

- faculty/staff endpoint and institutional IT support;
- LMS, course delivery, video and secure assessment;
- nursing-specific clinical placement, compliance and encounter tracking;
- simulation-center hardware, A/V capture, debriefing and EHR training environments;
- identity, security and institutional governance;
- faculty digital-competence and adoption constraints;
- current Shadow Health LTI 1.3 and faculty workflow fit;
- integration gaps to validate using internal customer, implementation and support data.

The work deliberately treats the nursing program as a federated technology architecture rather than assuming the LMS or simulation product is the system of record for the full learner journey.
