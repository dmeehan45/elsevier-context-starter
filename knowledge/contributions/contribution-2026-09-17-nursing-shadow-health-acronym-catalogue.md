# Contribution: nursing and Shadow Health acronym catalogue

Public-source research and synthesis created a living, role-specific language catalogue for Shadow Health product work.

The catalogue deliberately prioritizes the vocabulary most likely to affect product judgment rather than attempting to enumerate all clinical abbreviations. It covers:

- Shadow Health and HESI product language;
- nursing degree and career pathways;
- regulators, accreditors, and professional organizations;
- simulation terminology and credentials;
- clinical judgment, competency, and assessment frameworks;
- NCLEX / NGN terminology;
- selected clinical communication frameworks that recur in simulation;
- psychometrics;
- competitor vocabulary;
- learning technology and interoperability;
- privacy, security, accessibility, and institutional implementation;
- recognition-level international nursing regulation terminology.

Entries are classified as P0 (fluent), P1 (recognize immediately), or P2 (lookup/contextual), with explicit collision handling for ambiguous acronyms such as AACN, CNE, CE, SP, NRP, CRM, and CJMM/NCJMM.

The maintenance rule is to add terminology as it appears in real internal/customer/product work, preserve provenance, and promote terms in priority based on observed use rather than turning the document into a generic medical-abbreviation dictionary.
