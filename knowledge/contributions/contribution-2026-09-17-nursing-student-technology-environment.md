# Contribution: nursing student technology environment

Public-source research and synthesis covering:

- higher-education device ownership/access patterns;
- nursing-specific mobile-device evidence;
- current nursing-school laptop, tablet, smartphone, testing and connectivity requirements;
- BYOD and institution-provisioned-device models;
- clinical-setting restrictions on personal electronic devices;
- institution-provided simulation/telehealth hardware;
- current Shadow Health/Evolve technical requirements;
- existing Shadow Health client hardware telemetry and the internal questions it could answer.

The contribution deliberately separates device familiarity from device suitability and distinguishes what learners own from what institutions require and what clinical settings permit.
