# Contribution: Shadow Health marketing vs product documentation

Public-source comparison of how Elsevier positions Shadow Health externally versus how its support and training documentation describes product behavior.

The research separates outcome-oriented marketing constructs such as realism, clinical judgment, confidence, measurement, and practice readiness from the documented mechanics of course configuration, assignment activities, remediation, learner traces, scoring, proficiency, and faculty analytics.

The synthesis deliberately avoids treating either surface as complete ground truth. Marketing is used as evidence of buyer-facing positioning; support documentation is used as evidence of intended product mechanics; validation and actual outcome impact remain questions for empirical evidence and internal data.
