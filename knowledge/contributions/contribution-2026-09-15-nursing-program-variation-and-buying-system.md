# Contribution: nursing program variation and educational-technology buying system

Public-source research and synthesis extending the nursing-learning-journey work into:

- material variation among U.S. nursing-program operating models;
- multi-campus versus multi-program distinctions;
- international differences in clinical/practice-hour and simulation treatment;
- leadership archetypes from community-college through research-intensive and multi-campus institutions;
- nursing academic governance and budget authority;
- university technology, security, accessibility and procurement gates;
- a generalized educational-technology buying system for nursing;
- a testable hypothesis that operating-model attributes predict Shadow Health value/adoption more effectively than degree label or campus identity alone;
- explicit unresolved questions to validate using internal account data and customer evidence.

The work deliberately separates sourced formal structures and regulatory rules from hypotheses about actual influence, buying behavior and product-market fit.
