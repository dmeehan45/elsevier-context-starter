# Contribution: nursing program learning journey and simulation map

Public-source research and synthesis covering the U.S. nursing-education journey from admission/prerequisites through graduation, representative entry-level and advanced-practice program structures, the staff operating system around clinical education, and where simulation products fit across learner development.

The contribution deliberately avoids treating simulation as the organizing center of the nursing program. It frames simulation as one instructional and assessment methodology within a broader competency, clinical-placement, accreditation and practice-readiness system.
