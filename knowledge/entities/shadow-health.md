# Shadow Health

## Description

Elsevier's nursing-focused patient-encounter and clinical-simulation platform, originating from University of Florida virtual-human research and the Gainesville startup founded in 2011. Elsevier acquired Shadow Health in December 2020.

The original Digital Clinical Experiences product uses Digital Standardized Patients, a patented natural-language Conversation Engine, virtual examination/documentation workflows, and learner-performance measurement to let nursing learners practice patient communication and clinical reasoning in a safe, standardized setting.

In 2026 Elsevier expanded the Shadow Health brand with Shadow Health Lab with Virtual Reality, adding 165+ lab simulations, 30 VR patient encounters, SimChart documentation, facilitation/debriefing resources, and a multi-patient scenario.

## Current audiences

- undergraduate/prelicensure nursing students;
- graduate and advanced-practice nursing students;
- novice/new-graduate nurses;
- nursing faculty and program leadership;
- simulation educators and institutional buyers.
