# Shadow Health

## Description

Virtual-patient simulation business acquired by Elsevier in December 2020 and now part of the Healthcare Education portfolio. In 2026 Elsevier launched Shadow Health Lab with Virtual Reality, extending the product into immersive lab simulation and VR patient encounters.
