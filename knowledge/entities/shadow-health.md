# Shadow Health

## Description

Elsevier's nursing-focused patient-encounter and clinical-simulation platform, originating from University of Florida virtual-human research and the Gainesville startup founded in 2011. Elsevier acquired Shadow Health in December 2020.

The original Digital Clinical Experiences product uses Digital Standardized Patients, a patented natural-language Conversation Engine, virtual examination/documentation workflows, and learner-performance measurement to let nursing learners practice patient communication and clinical reasoning in a safe, standardized setting.

In 2026 Elsevier expanded the Shadow Health brand with Shadow Health Lab with Virtual Reality, adding 165+ lab simulations, 30 VR patient encounters, SimChart documentation, facilitation/debriefing resources, and a multi-patient scenario. Elsevier's 2026 launch language explicitly broadens Shadow Health toward a comprehensive patient-encounter solution across simulation modalities.

## Current audiences

- undergraduate/prelicensure nursing students;
- graduate and advanced-practice nursing students;
- novice/new-graduate nurses;
- nursing faculty and program leadership;
- simulation educators and institutional buyers.

## Positioning vs mechanics

Externally, Shadow Health is framed around realistic patient encounters, clinical reasoning/judgment, confidence, measurable learner growth, and practice readiness. Public support documentation reveals the underlying mechanism as configurable learning and assessment courseware: faculty-controlled assignment settings, structured patient-exam activities, remediation/repeat attempts, detailed learner traces, SPI and DCE scoring, proficiency bands, and faculty analytics.

Keep outcome claims and product measures distinct. In particular, SPI, DCE Score, proficiency, and the Practice Readiness Dashboard are related but not interchangeable constructs.
