# Elsevier's current AI products repeatedly combine trusted content with task-specific workflow

Across LeapSpace, ClinicalKey AI, Sherpath AI, Osmosis AI and Nora AI, Elsevier's public product framing emphasizes domain-trusted content, traceability/citations, human oversight or validation, and embedding AI inside a professional or learning workflow rather than offering a generic chatbot alone.

## Scope

Public product strategy visible as of 2026-09-14. This describes an observed cross-product pattern, not a confidential architecture standard.
