# Elsevier's acquisition pattern has expanded it from content into professional workflows

Across research and health, selected acquisitions have added capabilities around research administration, collaboration, repositories, publication workflow, semantic data, assessment, simulation, visual learning, patient engagement and EHR workflow. The current portfolio therefore reaches far beyond publication and reference content into the work people do before, during and after a decision.

## Scope

Interpretive synthesis of selected strategy-shaping acquisitions from 2001-2026. It is not a claim that acquisition is Elsevier's only or primary growth mechanism.

See [acquisition research bundle](../sources/elsevier-acquisition-history-2001-2026.md) and [current product catalog](../sources/elsevier-product-catalog-2026.md).

## Notes

The recurring strategic motif is content/data plus workflow plus analytics/AI. Treat this as synthesis, not an official Elsevier strategy statement.
