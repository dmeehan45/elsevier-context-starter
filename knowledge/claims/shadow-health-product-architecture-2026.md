# Shadow Health's core DCE combines open-language patient interaction, examination, documentation, reflection, and assessment

## Claim

As of 2026, Shadow Health Digital Clinical Experiences are primarily screen-based nursing simulations built around Digital Standardized Patients. Learners can interview patients using their own language through the patented Conversation Engine, perform virtual examinations, document in an EHR-style workflow, provide education/empathy, reason through findings, reflect, and receive detailed performance feedback.

Elsevier markets 80+ virtual patients across undergraduate and graduate nursing collections and reports use by more than 700,000 nursing students, though the public reach metric is not defined precisely enough to treat it as an annual-active-user count.
