# Elsevier acquired Shadow Health in December 2020

## Claim

Elsevier announced its acquisition of Shadow Health on December 9, 2020. At the time, Shadow Health reported use at more than 900 institutions in North America and roughly 200,000 students served during 2020. Public financial terms were not disclosed.

## Strategic scope

Elsevier explicitly linked the acquisition to digital healthcare education, communication and clinical reasoning, practice readiness, and its broader education-to-practice portfolio. The deal occurred during rapid pandemic-driven adoption of virtual clinical experiences.
