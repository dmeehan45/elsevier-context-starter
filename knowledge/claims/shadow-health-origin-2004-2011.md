# Shadow Health emerged from UF virtual-human communication research and became a company in 2011

## Claim

The technology behind Shadow Health grew out of University of Florida virtual-human research beginning around 2004. Shadow Health was co-founded in Gainesville in 2011 by Benjamin Lok, David Massias, and Aaron Kotranza after the technology was licensed from UF.

## Why it matters

The original problem was how to let healthcare learners safely practice patient communication, empathy, and clinical interaction. This history is important because it explains why natural-language patient conversation remains unusually central to Shadow Health's product identity.
