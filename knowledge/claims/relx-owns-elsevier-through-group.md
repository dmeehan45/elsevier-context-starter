# RELX owns Elsevier through RELX Group plc

RELX PLC is the sole parent company of RELX. It owns 100% of RELX Group plc, which in turn holds the group's operating businesses, including Elsevier.

## Scope

Current legal/corporate structure as publicly described by RELX on 2026-09-14.

## Notes
