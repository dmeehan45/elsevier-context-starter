# Elsevier's RELX segment generated £2.714bn of 2025 revenue

RELX reported 2025 Scientific, Technical & Medical revenue of £2.714 billion and adjusted operating profit of £1.035 billion.

## Scope

RELX's 2025 segment reporting. From 2025, print/print-related activities are reported separately and a small commercial-healthcare portfolio was moved to Risk, so comparisons with older segment figures require care.
