# Nursing simulation can replace some clinical experience, but quality and implementation are part of the requirement

## Claim

NCSBN's foundational simulation research demonstrated that high-quality simulation could substitute for up to 50% of traditional clinical hours in prelicensure nursing under appropriate program conditions. Current implementation remains subject to state, accreditation, institutional, faculty, and program requirements rather than a universal 50% entitlement.

INACSL's current standards emphasize simulation design, preparation, facilitation, debriefing, operations, measurable outcomes, evaluation, professional integrity, and faculty development.

## Product implication

For Shadow Health and its competitors, curriculum integration, faculty enablement, facilitation/debriefing tooling, analytics, and evidence are core adoption requirements when institutions want simulation to carry meaningful clinical-education weight.
