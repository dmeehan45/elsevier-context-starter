# Shadow Health is primarily a nursing-education B2B2C product with multiple learner segments

## Claim

Current public positioning centers on undergraduate nursing students, graduate/advanced-practice nursing students, and novice/new-graduate nurses. The decision and implementation audience includes nursing faculty, deans/directors, simulation leaders, and institutional purchasers.

Commercially, Shadow Health operates as a hybrid B2B2C courseware product: programs/faculty adopt and configure use in courses, while students may be required to purchase or redeem access codes/enrollment for assigned modules.

## Scope

Historically Shadow Health described itself more broadly across nursing and allied health. Current 2026 Elsevier marketing is materially more nursing-centric, so this claim describes current public positioning rather than the absolute technical addressable market.
