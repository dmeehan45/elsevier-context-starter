# Shadow Health turns interaction traces into learner-level and cohort-level performance signals

## Claim

Shadow Health does not only simulate encounters. It records learner actions and conversations, produces assignment feedback, and exposes structured measurements including the DCE Score, Student Performance Index (SPI), proficiency levels, and faculty analytics through a Practice Readiness Dashboard.

Elsevier recommends the normalized DCE score for grading. It describes SPI as a valid/reliable clinical-judgment measure but explicitly does not recommend using the raw SPI percentage directly for grading.

## Evidence boundary

The platform can measure performance inside its own simulation framework. Stronger claims that these measurements predict independent real-world practice readiness or patient outcomes require external validation beyond within-product performance and learner perception studies.
