# Nursing programs operate federated technology stacks rather than a single educational platform

## Claim

Current public nursing-school technology environments show separate institutional, academic, clinical and simulation technology layers with different workflows and support/ownership structures.

Representative components include:

- institutional endpoint/identity/productivity systems;
- LMS, video and secure assessment;
- publisher/learning content integrations;
- clinical placement, compliance and experience tracking;
- simulation hardware, A/V capture, debriefing and simulated EHR environments;
- analytics, competency and accreditation evidence.

No one system reliably represents the full learner journey.

## Implication

For a simulation technology provider, integration quality and reduction of cross-system faculty workload are part of product value. A platform that produces strong learning experiences but requires duplicate provisioning, grade transfer, identity reconciliation or manual evidence mapping can create meaningful operational cost for faculty and programs.
