# Claim

State one durable, scoped assertion.

## Scope

Clarify time, population, product, market, workflow, or other boundaries when needed.

## Evidence

Link the source(s) or observations that support the claim.

## Freshness

Use `volatility` and `review_after` when the assertion describes a current state that can become stale. Omit them for purely historical assertions when periodic review adds no value.

## Notes

Optional interpretation, caveats, or known counterevidence.
