# Hypothesis

State a proposition worth testing or monitoring.

## Why we think this

Link observations, claims, or reasoning that motivated it.

## What would change our mind

Describe useful confirming or disconfirming evidence.

## Evidence updates

Record important changes over time without rewriting the hypothesis as if it was always known.

## Freshness

Use `review_after` to keep open hypotheses from remaining active indefinitely after the surrounding evidence or decision context has changed.
